# Gezi AI brand

Gezi is a sharp, respectful business partner for shop owners. The identity is
quietly confident: a lowercase `gezi` wordmark paired with one geometric mark.
The default direction is concept 01, the rising loop.

## Mark construction

The mark combines a lowercase `g` silhouette with an upward chevron. The loop
represents a business understood clearly; the chevron represents growth that
comes from that clarity. Concept 01 is the production default. Concepts 02 and
03 remain in `gezi-logo-concepts.png` for founder review and can be swapped by
replacing the mark artwork as a family.

## Usage

- Keep clear space around the mark equal to the height of the mark's inner bar.
- Minimum digital mark size: 24px. Minimum lockup width: 112px.
- Use green-700 (`#0E6B4A`) on light surfaces and white on green-700/green-900.
- The wordmark is lowercase, geometric, custom-spaced, and never italicized.
- Keep the thermal receipt ASCII-only: `GEZI AI` remains text at the 32-character
  thermal width. The logo is reserved for future PDF documents and digital UI.

## Do / don't

Do use the mark with the lowercase wordmark, preserve clear space, and keep
green as the identity color. Don't add gradients, gold to the logo, mascots,
shields, lions, stock imagery, kitenge decoration, outlines, or extra symbols.
Don't stretch, rotate, recolor, or place the logo on busy photography. Gold is
for champion moments in product UI only; it is never a logo color.

## Product surfaces

The AppShell uses the lockup on desktop and the compact mark/wordmark treatment
on mobile. Login and signup use the mark. WhatsApp profile art is exported at
640×640. Thermal receipts keep ASCII `GEZI AI`; a future PDF receipt or
statement may use the lockup as a digital document header.
